01. UX Research & Discovery

Target User Persona & Problem Statement

Designed specifically around the urgent needs of senior software engineering students facing strict university registration deadlines.

Primary Persona

Nguyen Van Minh

20 Years Old • FPT University Senior (SE 7th Semester)
Daily Context Attends campus lectures 4 days/week, works part-time backend 15h/week. Relies heavily on mobile notifications.
Primary Device Xiaomi 13 (Android, 1080x2400, viewport ~392dp). One-handed thumb use on campus bus/cafeteria.
Accessibility Needs Mild astigmatism; low-contrast gray text is illegible under campus sunlight. Requires minimum 48×48dp touch targets.

Core Goals vs. Pain Points

Goals & Desired Outcomes
  • Discover Capstone groups looking for Backend/AI roles before the hard deadline.
  • Quickly inspect group members' tech stack, GitHub, and experience.
  • Elect team leader seamlessly and democratically.
  • Know with 100% certainty that the team fulfills the legal 4–5 member regulation before locking.
Critical Pain Points
  • Unclear Availability: Chaotic chat groups and outdated spreadsheets result in applying to full groups.
  • Deadlock on Leadership: Teams don't know who has the authority to submit and lock the final group roster.
  • Risk of Invalid Rosters: Accidentally locking with only 3 members results in forced randomization by admin.
One-Sentence Problem Statement
"Capstone students struggle to find compatible, available teammates, track group capacity (4–5 members), and coordinate leader assignment before the strict semester registration deadline."
Measurable Success Criterion
"A student can discover and join an eligible group with open slots in under 60 seconds, and a team leader can verify roster compliance and execute the group lock action in under 15 seconds without encountering unexpected validation errors."

Flow 1: Discover & Join an Open Capstone Group

Primary Flow
1. Start at Dashboard SCR_02

Minh inspects deadline countdown and taps "Browse Available Teams" or bottom navigation tab.

2. Browse Groups Feed & Apply Filters SCR_03

Applies filter chip "Open Slots Only" + tag "AI/ML". Group cards display 3 filled avatars + 2 dashed open slots.

3. Inspect Group Detail & Members SCR_04

Minh examines members' technical roles, GitHub links, and sees slot 4 seeks Backend developer. Taps "Request to Join Team".

4. Real-Time Capacity Validation & Join Confirmation SCR_04 ➔ SCR_05

Happy Path: Capacity < 5 members ➔ Instant join confirmation modal ➔ Toast "Successfully joined Team AI-04!" ➔ Redirected to My Group Hub.
Alternative Path: If slot was taken concurrently (5/5), toast alert "Group reached capacity!" and refresh list without crashing.

Flow 2: Democratic Team Leader Voting

Governance Flow
1. Notification & Banner Trigger SCR_05

My Group Hub displays prominent alert banner: "Leader not yet elected — Vote now to unlock group locking." Taps "Vote for Leader".

2. Select Candidate Card SCR_06

Selects a peer candidate using high-affordance 64dp cards with 2dp orange border and checkmark circle. Sees live vote count.

3. Submit & Tally Resolution SCR_06 ➔ SCR_05

Taps "Submit My Vote". If candidate reaches simple majority (e.g., 3/5 votes), system immediately crowns leader with Crown badge 👑 in Group Hub and unlocks the "Lock Roster" action.

Flow 3: Group Roster Lock (Error Prevention & Recovery Path)

Compliance Critical
1. Elected Leader Initiates Lock SCR_05 ➔ SCR_07

Only the elected leader has authority to tap "Proceed to Lock Team", opening the Lock Review Screen.

2. Error State (< 4 Members Underflow) SCR_07 Error

System Validation: If team has only 3 members, primary "Lock Team Roster" button is DISABLED. Displays high-contrast red alert banner (#BA1A1A on #FDF2F2): "Ineligible to Lock Roster: FPT Capstone regulations require at least 4 members."

3. Recovery Action: Invite from Waiting Pool Recovery Action

Inline Recovery Card directly below the error: "Need 1 more member? Invite unassigned peers from the Random Pool." Leader taps "Invite from Pool" with 1 click to fill the 4th slot instead of being blocked in a dead end.

4. Destructive Confirmation & Final Lock SCR_07 Modal

When team reaches 4–5 members, leader taps active "Lock Team Roster". A destructive confirmation modal appears: "This action cannot be undone. Roster will be finalized for Registrar submission." Leader confirms ➔ Permanent LOCKED badge displayed with official submission timestamp.

Iteration 01

Ambiguous Group Capacity & Hidden Action Affordance

Usability Fix
BEFORE (Initial AI Generation) ❌ FAILED
Identified Flaw: Group card used tiny plain text for "3/5" without visually distinguishing open slots from filled slots. Tap area was ambiguous and looked like static text.
AFTER (Refined with Prompt) ✅ RESOLVED
Design Improvement: Added distinct green status badge ("3/5 Slots Filled - 2 Open"), 5 explicit avatar circles (3 filled, 2 dashed open slots), and a 48dp high-affordance CTA button.
Exact Prompt Sent to Google Stitch:
Refine the Group Cards in SCR_03 (Browse Groups): 1. Add a distinct visual badge in top-right corner: Green background (#E6F4EA) with text #0D8244 "3/5 Slots Filled - 2 Open". 2. For full groups (5/5), show gray badge "Full (5/5)" and disable CTA. 3. Include 5 avatar circles in a row: 3 filled student avatars + 2 gray dashed placeholder circles with "+" icon. 4. Primary button labeled "View Details & Join" with min 48dp height and #F27024 background.
Iteration 02

Missing Error State & Ineligible Lock Feedback (< 4 Members)

Critical Compliance
BEFORE (Initial AI Generation) ❌ FALSE POSITIVE
Identified Flaw: Tapping "Lock Team Roster" on a 3-person team showed a generic success modal. This violates FPT University regulations requiring at least 4 members.
AFTER (Refined with Error & Recovery) ✅ ERROR PREVENTED
Design Improvement: Prominent Error Alert Banner (#BA1A1A on #FDF2F2), disabled lock button, and an actionable Recovery Card: "Need 1 more member? Invite from Waiting Pool".
Exact Prompt Sent to Google Stitch:
Generate error and recovery state for SCR_07 (Lock Review) when team has only 3/5 members: 1. Display prominent Error Alert Banner: background #FDF2F2, border #BA1A1A, bold text: "Ineligible to Lock Roster: Your team has 3 members. FPT Capstone regulations require at least 4 members." 2. Disable CTA button "Lock Team Roster" (gray background #E1E3E5). 3. Add Recovery Card: "Need 1 more member? You can invite unassigned students from Waiting Pool" with secondary button "Invite from Pool".
Iteration 03

Low Contrast & Small Touch Target (< 48dp) in Leader Voting

WCAG 2.1 AA Fix
BEFORE (Initial AI Generation) ❌ CONTRAST FAIL
Identified Flaw: Candidate selection used tiny 18×18dp radio buttons with low-contrast gray text (#9E9E9E, ratio 2.8:1 - FAIL) that caused mis-taps under bright campus sunlight.
AFTER (Refined Interactive Cards) ✅ PASS WCAG AA (5.4:1)
Design Improvement: Transformed into 64dp full-width selectable cards with 2dp orange border, high-contrast typography (5.4:1), 24dp checkmark circle, and real-time vote tally pill.
Exact Prompt Sent to Google Stitch:
Redesign Screen SCR_06 (Leader Voting): 1. Turn each candidate into an interactive Selectable Card with minimum height of 64dp (exceeding 48x48dp target). 2. Increase text contrast: Candidate name in 16sp bold (#1A1D1E - 15.6:1), role in 14sp (#596066 - 5.4:1). 3. Selected state features 2dp solid border in #F27024, light orange background tint (#FFF1E8), and large 24dp checkmark circle. 4. Display real-time vote tally pills (e.g., "2 votes received") using #0D8244 for front-runner.
05. UX Evaluation

Nielsen 10 Heuristics & WCAG 2.1 Audit

Rigorous heuristic inspection of all 9 screens with documented decisions (Accepted / Modified / Rejected).

ID Screen Standard / Heuristic Finding Description Severity Decision Design Action & Justification
F-01 SCR_07 (Lock Review) Heuristic #5: Error Prevention Locking dialog had a single "OK" button without explaining that locking is permanent and irreversibly closes roster editing. High ACCEPTED Redesigned dialog into a Destructive Confirmation Dialog with bold red warning text and dual buttons ("Cancel" vs "Yes, Lock Permanently").
F-02 SCR_03 (Browse Groups) WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.3: Contrast (Minimum) Secondary tech tags used light gray text #A0A5AA on #F0F1F2 (2.3:1 contrast, failing 4.5:1 minimum). High ACCEPTED Updated tag token to use background #E9ECEF with text #343A40, achieving a contrast ratio of 7.2:1 (exceeds AAA).
F-03 SCR_05 (My Group) Heuristic #1: Visibility of System Status When a student requests to join, there was no indicator showing whether application was "Pending", "Accepted", or "Rejected". Medium ACCEPTED Added persistent Status Pill on SCR_05: Yellow badge with clock icon ⏳ "Pending Leader Review (Submitted 2h ago)".
F-04 SCR_06 (Leader Voting) WCAG 2.1 SC 2.5.5: Target Size The "Change Vote" link was rendered as an inline 12sp text link with an effective touch area of only 24×14dp. High MODIFIED Converted into Outlined Button with padding 12dp 16dp and min-height 48dp, displayed only while the voting window is still open.
F-05 SCR_02 (Dashboard) Heuristic #6: Recognition Rather Than Recall Dashboard displayed deadline as raw date without a relative countdown, forcing students to calculate mental hours. Medium ACCEPTED Replaced static date with a Dynamic Countdown Card: "⏳ 2 Days 14 Hours Left" with color change to warning orange at <48h.
